## Full description (4000 char max)

MergeBlox is a fast, satisfying number-merging puzzle. No swiping, no timers pressuring you — just tap a tile, then tap a matching neighbor to merge them into something bigger.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap any tile to select it, then tap an adjacent tile with the same number to merge them into double the value. New tiles appear as you play. Keep merging to reach higher numbers and rack up your score before the board fills up.

WHY MERGEBLOX
• Simple one-tap controls — easy to pick up, hard to put down
• Clean, colorful design with no clutter
• Quick sessions — perfect for a coffee break or a commute
• Watch a short ad to clear space and keep a great run going when the board fills up
• Chase your best score, one merge at a time

Whether you have thirty seconds or thirty minutes, MergeBlox is built for quick, satisfying play.
